### Full Description
Le Palme is a highly-regarded restaurant run by two brothers, Chef Armando and Hall Master Salvatore. During peak tourist season and school holidays, Salvatore's children lend a hand in the dining area. Since 1990, these brothers have been dedicated to winning over every guest with their impeccable service and delicious cuisine.

On the Amalfi Coast, many restaurants struggle with proper meat preparation. However, Le Palme stands out as an exception with every dish expertly crafted, whether it's the signature pasta with pistachio pesto or paccheri with redfish and walnuts. Don't miss out on their grilled seafood or homemade pork medallions, made using a family recipe. Armando and Salvatore stay current with the latest trends and are able to converse with guests in any language. In addition to traditional dishes, they also offer gluten-free pasta and pizza options.

The atmosphere at Le Palme is always lively, friendly, and fun, with generous portions and a great selection of wines. Salvatore, in addition to managing the restaurant, also oversees a rental villa, striving to provide unparalleled comfort and convenience for his guests. Whether dining in or taking out, the brothers are always accommodating and eager to please.
